My journey

Learning by building

I picked up a crochet hook a few years ago mostly out of curiosity, and it turned into something more — a small business I called By Praise. At its busiest, it was bringing in over ₦100,000 a month in sales, all from products I made and sold myself: mini bags, tote bags, earrings, scrunchies, hair bows, and more.

"I didn't set out to build a business. I set out to make something with my hands — the business part I learned along the way."

Right now I'm rebuilding By Praise properly, with a clearer brand and plan, ahead of an official online launch in 2027 — the same year I graduate high school. In between, I'm an online student exploring business, technology, research, and leadership, and slowly picking up the digital skills I know I'll need for what comes next.
